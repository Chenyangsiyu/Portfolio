Art direction · Immersive experience · 2025

IKEA

Once upon a time… a mattress

A five-minute immersive experience where IKEA’s idea of perfect rest comes alive through a fairy tale: lying in the bed of their dreams, visitors find a bedroom turned into a sensory space.

Team
Group project of three, with Léo-Amati Tambwa Kalombo and Weiyi Yang: every stage done together
Deliverables
Concept, visitor journey, app, visual identity, communication
Venue & dates
Pavillon de l’Arsenal, Paris · 3–17 June 2025

01 — The concept

Turning an everyday need into a fairy tale.

IKEA imagines a Temporary Art Zone (ZAT): a pop-up space that makes room for creativity while carrying the brand’s values. Our answer is a reminder that perfect comfort is something you live, feel and dream. Not a grand epic, just a small, intimate, reassuring tale.

“Once upon a time… a mattress. Not too firm, not too soft. Not too warm, not too cold. Just right.”

02 — Visitor journey

Three steps, from the door to the story.

  1. 1

    Welcome and access

    Entry to the Pavillon de l’Arsenal is free. IKEA guides direct each visitor: without a booking, they help pick a room based on bed preferences and open slots; with a booking, visitors wait right outside their assigned room.

  2. 2

    Four rooms, four stories

    The experience unfolds across four giant IKEA boxes, each marking the entrance to a room devoted to one tale: Little Red Riding Hood, Jack and the Beanstalk, Goldilocks and the Three Bears, and The Princess and the Pea.

  3. 3

    Into the tale

    Visitors lie down on an IKEA bed in the middle of the room. 360° projections and headphones carry them into the story for about five minutes.

03 — The app

A quiz to find your story.

After downloading the app, users answer a quiz about their lifestyle and sleep habits. Based on their answers, the app suggests an immersive tale, which they then experience in the physical ZAT.

04 — Four rooms, four tales

Each room combines three immersive techniques: 3D sound that moves around the visitor, localised vibrations in the mattress, and LED lighting that follows the story.

05 — Visual identity

A set of pictograms drawn from the four tales, the hood, the beanstalk, the bear and the pea, combined into patterns in a bright palette inspired by IKEA. Paris 2024 for headlines, Futura for text.
