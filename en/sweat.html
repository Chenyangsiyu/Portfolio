Product design · Mobile app

Sweat

An app to quickly find and book a place to play sport close to home.

Contribution
Research, personas, concept, prototype and identity
Type
School project · Mobile app
Data
Data ES · French Ministry of Sport

01 — The problem

Doing sport is easy. Finding where to do it, much less so.

Information about gyms and courts is scattered across websites and posters. Finding a slot often means comparing, calling and starting over.

Problem statement

How might we help people quickly find a suitable, reliable place to play sport close to home, without wasting time on scattered websites or calling different venues?

02 — The data

Built on an official, reliable source.

The app relies on Data ES, the French Ministry of Sport’s official database of sports facilities.

03 — Personas

Two profiles, one shared wish: get moving without the hassle.

A student who wants to play sport with friends, and an employee looking for a routine close to home. Their blockers overlap: little visibility, price, scattered information.

Sarah Dupont

22 · film student · Paris

“For me, sport is mostly a way to spend time with my friends and switch off from classes.”

Frustrations

  • Limited or no sports facilities at her school
  • Prices too high for a student budget
  • Information scattered across websites and posters

Needs

  • Easily book courts to play with friends
  • Find accessible, affordable venues in Paris
  • A variety of team sports

Julien Martin

42 · communications officer · Val-de-Marne

“After work, I’d like a simple routine to exercise and stick to it every day.”

Frustrations

  • Little visibility on facilities near home
  • Memberships that are expensive or too rigid

Needs

  • Sports venues less than 30 minutes from home
  • Varied activities so he doesn’t get bored
  • Clear information: price, opening hours, accessibility

04 — The concept

Find a slot near you in a few seconds.

Using Data ES, the app shows available slots at nearby gyms and courts. Tennis, boxing, squash… all the information is in one place to make booking easier.

User need

Sweat’s answer

Nearby, accessible venues

Location-based search on the Data ES database

Clear info: price, hours, accessibility

All the information in one place

Easy booking, alone or with friends

Available slots, bookable directly

Variety of activities

Filters by sport: tennis, boxing, squash…
